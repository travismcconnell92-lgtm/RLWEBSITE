Construction & Business Attorney Serving DeLand

DeLand is the Volusia County seat and a university town, and its downtown is one of the better preserved in Central Florida.

Ready Legal's office is in downtown Orlando, Florida. We do not maintain an office in DeLand. Ready Legal represents clients throughout Central Florida, including DeLand and the rest of Volusia County.

Building in DeLand

The historic commercial core has been kept in continuous use, which means the buildings have been adapted repeatedly. Restaurant and retail build-outs, upper-floor conversions and facade work all take place in structures whose actual condition is only discovered once work begins. The university adds institutional construction and the residential demand that comes with it.

As the county seat, DeLand also carries government and civic construction and the professional offices around it, which is procured and administered with more formality than private work.

A university town's building cycle

Institutional work runs on an academic calendar, which means substantial projects are compressed into the months when buildings are empty. Compressed schedules on older structures are a reliable source of the same problem: the work uncovers something, the schedule has no room to absorb it, and the parties end up arguing about acceleration and delay.

How Ready Legal helps

Ready Legal works with owners and contractors on renovation and remodeling work as well as new construction. That includes drafting and negotiating construction contracts before work begins, and handling payment disputes, lien claims, defect claims and construction litigation when a project goes wrong.

DeLand businesses

Downtown DeLand supports a dense cluster of independent restaurants, retail and service businesses in leased historic space. Ready Legal also represents businesses on formation, contracts, commercial disputes and collections.

Courts serving DeLand

DeLand sits in Volusia County, which is served by Florida's Seventh Judicial Circuit. Litigation arising out of DeLand disputes is generally handled in Volusia County. Ready Legal's office is in downtown Orlando and the firm is available to represent clients in matters in the Seventh Judicial Circuit.
