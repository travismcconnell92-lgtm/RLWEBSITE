Construction & Business Attorney Serving Sarasota

Sarasota builds at the high end, on the water, and the expectations that come with that shape every project.

Ready Legal's office is in downtown Orlando, Florida. We do not maintain an office in Sarasota. Ready Legal represents clients throughout Central Florida, including Sarasota and the rest of Sarasota County.

Building in Sarasota

The barrier islands and the waterfront neighborhoods support custom residential construction at budgets and specification levels well above the regional norm, along with a substantial condominium market. Coastal construction requirements, elevation, and the durability demands of a marine environment all add cost and complexity before a single design decision is made.

High-specification work carries high-specification disputes. Owners are engaged and exacting, allowances and selections drive cost, and the difference between a defect and a disappointment is often a question about what the contract documents actually required.

Condominium and multi-unit work

Alongside the custom market sits a large stock of multi-unit coastal buildings requiring substantial ongoing work to envelopes, balconies and structure. Those projects are commissioned by associations, funded by owners and delivered by contractors on occupied buildings, which is a combination that produces disputes about scope, cost and responsibility as a matter of course.

How Ready Legal helps

Ready Legal works with homeowners, builders and subcontractors on residential construction matters. That includes drafting and negotiating construction contracts before work begins, and handling payment disputes, lien claims, defect claims and construction litigation when a project goes wrong. Ready Legal is available to represent clients in Sarasota County matters.

Courts serving Sarasota

Sarasota sits in Sarasota County, which is served by Florida's Twelfth Judicial Circuit. Litigation arising out of Sarasota disputes is generally handled in Sarasota County. Ready Legal's office is in downtown Orlando and the firm is available to represent clients in matters in the Twelfth Judicial Circuit.
