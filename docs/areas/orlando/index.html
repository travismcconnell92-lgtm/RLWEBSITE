Ready Legal's office is in downtown Orlando, Florida. Ready Legal represents clients throughout Central Florida.

Building in Orlando

Orlando is the commercial center of the region and almost every kind of construction happens here at once. Downtown office and mixed-use towers. The hospitality corridor along International Drive, where hotels and attractions are in a near-permanent cycle of building, renovating and rebuilding. Medical and institutional work. Industrial and distribution space pushing out along the beltway. And residential construction in every direction, from infill teardowns in the older neighborhoods to entire subdivisions on the edge of the county.

That mix means deep contractor tiers. A single job can involve a national general contractor, a dozen subcontractors, sub-subcontractors under them, and suppliers none of whom have ever spoken to the owner. Money moves down that chain slowly, and when it stops moving, it usually stops for everyone below the point where it stopped.

How Ready Legal helps

Ready Legal works with owners, contractors, subcontractors and suppliers on construction matters. That includes drafting and negotiating construction contracts before work starts, and handling payment disputes, lien claims, defect claims and construction litigation when a project goes wrong.

The firm also represents Orlando businesses on formation, contracts, commercial disputes and collections.

Orlando businesses

Orlando's business base runs from contractors and trade companies to restaurants, agencies, professional firms and the long tail of small companies that serve the tourism economy. Many of them operate on thin margins and on other people's payment terms, which is a combination that produces contract disputes and collection problems.

Ready Legal represents Orlando businesses on formation, contract drafting and review, commercial disputes, collections and litigation.

Courts serving Orlando

Orlando sits in Orange County, which is served by Florida's Ninth Judicial Circuit. Litigation arising out of Orlando disputes is generally handled in Orange County. Ready Legal's office is in downtown Orlando and the firm is available to represent clients in matters in the Ninth Judicial Circuit.
