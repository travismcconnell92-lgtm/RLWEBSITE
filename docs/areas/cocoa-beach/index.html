Construction & Business Attorney Serving Cocoa Beach

Cocoa Beach is a narrow barrier island lined with buildings that mostly went up in the same two decades.

Ready Legal's office is in downtown Orlando, Florida. We do not maintain an office in Cocoa Beach. Ready Legal represents clients throughout Central Florida, including Cocoa Beach and the rest of Brevard County.

Building in Cocoa Beach

The condominium and hotel stock along the shore dates largely from the 1960s and 1970s, and buildings of that age in that environment need serious and expensive work: roofs, envelopes, balconies, railings, concrete restoration. There is very little land left, so construction is overwhelmingly renovation, repair and replacement rather than new building.

That work is governed by associations, funded by unit owners and performed by contractors on occupied buildings. Disagreements about scope, cost, quality and who is responsible for which component are a normal feature of it rather than an exception.

Association work and unit owners

Major building work in a condominium is a construction project and a community decision at the same time. It has to be scoped, funded and approved before a contractor is engaged, and the people paying for it are not the people managing it. That structure produces disagreements about necessity, about cost, about the quality of what was delivered, and about which components were the association's responsibility in the first place.

How Ready Legal helps

Ready Legal works with owners, associations, contractors and subcontractors on construction matters. That includes drafting and negotiating construction contracts before work begins, and handling payment disputes, lien claims, defect claims and construction litigation when a project goes wrong. That includes construction disputes involving multi-unit and association-governed buildings.

Courts serving Cocoa Beach

Cocoa Beach sits in Brevard County, which is served by Florida's Eighteenth Judicial Circuit. Litigation arising out of Cocoa Beach disputes is generally handled in Brevard County. Ready Legal's office is in downtown Orlando and the firm is available to represent clients in matters in the Eighteenth Judicial Circuit.
