Construction & Business Attorney Serving Tavares

Tavares is the Lake County seat, and a county seat has a construction profile of its own.

Ready Legal's office is in downtown Orlando, Florida. We do not maintain an office in Tavares. Ready Legal represents clients throughout Central Florida, including Tavares and the rest of Lake County.

Building in Tavares

Government and civic buildings, courthouse facilities, offices for the professionals who work around them, and the parking and infrastructure to serve all of it make up a meaningful share of what gets built downtown. Public and institutional work is procured differently and administered differently than private work, with more formality about changes, submittals and payment.

The waterfront and the lakefront neighborhoods add residential and hospitality construction, including the seaplane and marine activity the city has built its identity around. Waterfront work brings docks, seawalls and shoreline structures, which sit in their own permitting and construction category.

Working around the lakes

A large share of local property fronts water, which puts docks, boat lifts, seawalls and shoreline work into the ordinary run of construction here. That work is permitted separately, built by contractors who do that work specifically, and exposed to conditions that punish shortcuts. When it fails it usually fails in a way that is expensive and obvious, and the argument is about whether it was built to what the contract described.

How Ready Legal helps

Ready Legal works with owners, contractors and subcontractors on commercial construction matters. That includes drafting and negotiating construction contracts before work begins, and handling payment disputes, lien claims, defect claims and construction litigation when a project goes wrong.

Tavares businesses

Ready Legal also represents businesses on formation, contracts, commercial disputes and collections.

Courts serving Tavares

Tavares sits in Lake County, which is served by Florida's Fifth Judicial Circuit. Litigation arising out of Tavares disputes is generally handled in Lake County. Ready Legal's office is in downtown Orlando and the firm is available to represent clients in matters in the Fifth Judicial Circuit.
