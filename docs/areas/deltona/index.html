Construction & Business Attorney Serving Deltona

Deltona was platted as an enormous residential subdivision long before it became a city, and it has been filling in one lot at a time ever since.

Ready Legal's office is in downtown Orlando, Florida. We do not maintain an office in Deltona. Ready Legal represents clients throughout Central Florida, including Deltona and the rest of Volusia County.

Building in Deltona

Because the lots were laid out decades ago and sold individually, construction has happened piecemeal rather than in coordinated phases. Houses of very different ages and standards sit next to each other, infill building continues on lots that were never developed, and drainage and stormwater remain a recurring issue across the city.

That produces two kinds of work at once: new single-lot construction, often by smaller builders, and substantial renovation of a large older housing stock. Both tend to involve owners dealing directly with a contractor, on paperwork that is thinner than the project warrants.

How Ready Legal helps

Ready Legal works with homeowners, builders and subcontractors on residential construction matters. That includes drafting and negotiating construction contracts before work begins, and handling payment disputes, lien claims, defect claims and construction litigation when a project goes wrong. On residential work of this kind, a great deal turns on what the written agreement actually said before the first payment was made.

Courts serving Deltona

Deltona sits in Volusia County, which is served by Florida's Seventh Judicial Circuit. Litigation arising out of Deltona disputes is generally handled in Volusia County. Ready Legal's office is in downtown Orlando and the firm is available to represent clients in matters in the Seventh Judicial Circuit.
