Construction & Business Attorney Serving St. Petersburg

St. Petersburg is a waterfront city with almost no room left, so nearly everything built there replaces or improves something else.

Ready Legal's office is in downtown Orlando, Florida. We do not maintain an office in St. Petersburg. Ready Legal represents clients throughout Central Florida, including St. Petersburg and the rest of Pinellas County.

Building in St. Petersburg

Downtown redevelopment has added residential towers and mixed-use buildings on a peninsula that was already built out, which puts construction on tight urban sites with limited access and neighbors on every side. Beyond the core, an older housing stock across the city drives a large renovation market.

The waterfront and barrier island condominium buildings are their own category. Structures of significant age in a marine environment require ongoing work to roofs, envelopes, balconies and concrete, funded and managed by associations on behalf of owners who do not always agree about it.

Building on a built-out peninsula

Construction on tight urban sites is constrained before design begins. Staging, access, crane placement, neighboring structures and utilities all limit how the work can be sequenced, and those constraints drive cost and schedule far more than the building itself does. Delay claims on projects like that usually come down to who was responsible for a condition that everyone could see from the street.

How Ready Legal helps

Ready Legal works with owners, associations, contractors and subcontractors on construction matters. That includes drafting and negotiating construction contracts before work begins, and handling payment disputes, lien claims, defect claims and construction litigation when a project goes wrong. That includes construction disputes involving multi-unit and association-governed buildings.

Courts serving St. Petersburg

St. Petersburg sits in Pinellas County, which is served by Florida's Sixth Judicial Circuit. Litigation arising out of St. Petersburg disputes is generally handled in Pinellas County. Ready Legal's office is in downtown Orlando and the firm is available to represent clients in matters in the Sixth Judicial Circuit.
