Construction & Business Attorney Serving Winter Park

Winter Park is one of Central Florida's older established communities, and the building that happens there reflects it.

Ready Legal's office is in downtown Orlando, Florida. We do not maintain an office in Winter Park. Ready Legal represents clients throughout Central Florida, including Winter Park and the rest of Orange County.

Building in Winter Park

Much of the work is renovation rather than new construction. Additions, kitchen and bath remodels, pool and outdoor projects, and full rebuilds on lots where the original house is worth less than the land it sits on. The homes are in established neighborhoods with mature trees and tight setbacks, and in some areas there is additional review before a shovel goes in the ground, so a project that would be routine elsewhere involves more approvals and more moving parts.

Renovation work of that kind tends to be built on relationships more than paperwork. An owner hires a builder they were referred to, the scope grows once the work uncovers what is behind the walls, and at some point the written contract stops describing the project that is actually being built. Disagreements tend to start there, usually about money, change orders, delay, or the quality of finished work.

How Ready Legal helps

Ready Legal works with both owners and contractors on construction matters. That includes drafting and negotiating construction contracts before work begins, and handling payment disputes, lien claims, defect claims and construction litigation when a project goes wrong.

Winter Park businesses

Winter Park also carries a dense concentration of small businesses, along Park Avenue and through the office corridors around it. Professional firms, agencies, restaurants, retail and service companies, most of them operating out of leased space. Ready Legal represents small businesses on formation, contracts, commercial disputes and collections.

Courts serving Winter Park

Winter Park sits in Orange County, which is served by Florida's Ninth Judicial Circuit. Litigation arising out of Winter Park disputes is generally handled in Orange County. Ready Legal's office is in downtown Orlando and the firm is available to represent clients in matters in the Ninth Judicial Circuit.
