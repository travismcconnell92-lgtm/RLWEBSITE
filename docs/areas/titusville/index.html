Construction & Business Attorney Serving Titusville

Titusville sits directly across the river from the space center, and its fortunes and its construction have always followed what happens there.

Ready Legal's office is in downtown Orlando, Florida. We do not maintain an office in Titusville. Ready Legal represents clients throughout Central Florida, including Titusville and the rest of Brevard County.

Building in Titusville

Renewed launch activity has brought industrial, logistics and support facilities back to the area, along with residential development to house the people working in them. That is a genuine change from a long stretch when the city was building very little, and it means a lot of current construction is happening on or alongside infrastructure built for an earlier era.

The older downtown and the riverfront are a separate, smaller market of renovation and adaptive reuse, where existing conditions and long-deferred maintenance shape the scope of any project.

Building alongside older infrastructure

Much of the industrial and commercial land in the area was developed for an earlier generation of activity, so new projects frequently sit on sites with existing utilities, foundations, and conditions that were documented loosely or not at all. Unforeseen site conditions are the single most reliable source of change orders on work like that, and how the contract allocated that risk usually decides who pays for it.

How Ready Legal helps

Ready Legal works with owners, contractors and subcontractors on commercial and industrial construction matters. That includes drafting and negotiating construction contracts before work begins, and handling payment disputes, lien claims, defect claims and construction litigation when a project goes wrong.

Courts serving Titusville

Titusville sits in Brevard County, which is served by Florida's Eighteenth Judicial Circuit. Litigation arising out of Titusville disputes is generally handled in Brevard County. Ready Legal's office is in downtown Orlando and the firm is available to represent clients in matters in the Eighteenth Judicial Circuit.
