Construction & Business Attorney Serving Altamonte Springs

Altamonte Springs is the commercial hub of south Seminole County, and most of what gets built there is built on top of something older.

Ready Legal's office is in downtown Orlando, Florida. We do not maintain an office in Altamonte Springs. Ready Legal represents clients throughout Central Florida, including Altamonte Springs and the rest of Seminole County.

Building in Altamonte Springs

The retail spine along 436 and the area around Cranes Roost has been developed, redeveloped and repositioned for decades. Big-box boxes become something else. Hotels get renovated. Aging commercial buildings get gutted and re-tenanted. Alongside that there is a steady volume of apartment and multifamily construction, both new and the kind that replaces something from an earlier era.

Redevelopment work carries risks that ground-up construction does not. Existing conditions turn out not to match the drawings, unforeseen structural or site issues surface mid-project, and schedules slip in ways nobody priced. Those are the projects where change orders pile up and the parties end up arguing about who carried the risk of what was already there.

How Ready Legal helps

Ready Legal works with owners, contractors and subcontractors on construction matters, including drafting and negotiating construction contracts before work begins, and handling payment disputes, lien claims, defect claims and construction litigation.

The firm also represents Seminole County businesses on formation, contracts, commercial disputes and collections.

Multifamily and hospitality

Apartment and hotel work in the corridor tends to run on tight schedules with heavy subcontractor tiers, because a unit that is not finished is a unit that is not earning. Delay becomes the central issue rather than a side issue, and the argument about who caused it usually starts long before anyone files anything.

Ready Legal handles construction contract, payment, lien, defect and delay disputes for owners, contractors and subcontractors on projects of that kind.

Courts serving Altamonte Springs

Altamonte Springs sits in Seminole County, which is served by Florida's Eighteenth Judicial Circuit. Litigation arising out of Altamonte Springs disputes is generally handled in Seminole County. Ready Legal's office is in downtown Orlando and the firm is available to represent clients in matters in the Eighteenth Judicial Circuit.
