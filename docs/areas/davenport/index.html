Construction & Business Attorney Serving Davenport

Davenport sits at the edge of the tourist corridor, and a great deal of what is built there is built to be rented rather than lived in.

Ready Legal's office is in downtown Orlando, Florida. We do not maintain an office in Davenport. Ready Legal represents clients throughout Central Florida, including Davenport and the rest of Polk County.

Building in Davenport

Entire subdivisions in the area are developed as short-term rental communities: larger homes with more bedrooms than a family needs, pools on most lots, and management infrastructure built into the community from the start. Buyers are frequently investors, often from out of state or out of the country, purchasing from plans and relying on people they will never meet in person.

That distance is the recurring theme in disputes here. An owner who is not local has limited ability to watch the work, inspect what is covered up, or press a builder in real time. Problems tend to be identified later than they would be by a resident owner, and by then the question is what the contract and the warranty actually provide.

How Ready Legal helps

Ready Legal works with homeowners, builders and subcontractors on residential construction matters. That includes drafting and negotiating construction contracts before work begins, and handling payment disputes, lien claims, defect claims and construction litigation when a project goes wrong.

Davenport businesses

A large part of the local economy exists to service short-term rental property: management, cleaning, maintenance, pool and landscape companies and the trades behind them. Ready Legal also represents businesses on formation, contracts, commercial disputes and collections.

Courts serving Davenport

Davenport sits in Polk County, which is served by Florida's Tenth Judicial Circuit. Litigation arising out of Davenport disputes is generally handled in Polk County. Ready Legal's office is in downtown Orlando and the firm is available to represent clients in matters in the Tenth Judicial Circuit.
