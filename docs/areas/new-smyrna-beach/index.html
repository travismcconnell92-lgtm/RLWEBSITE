Construction & Business Attorney Serving New Smyrna Beach

New Smyrna Beach has been replacing its old beach cottages with much larger buildings for years, and that transition drives most of the construction there.

Ready Legal's office is in downtown Orlando, Florida. We do not maintain an office in New Smyrna Beach. Ready Legal represents clients throughout Central Florida, including New Smyrna Beach and the rest of Volusia County.

Building in New Smyrna Beach

Small mid-century beach houses on valuable lots get demolished and rebuilt at several times the size, and multi-unit and condominium buildings continue to go up along the shoreline. Both put substantial new construction into an environment that is hard on buildings, on lots where setbacks, elevation and coastal construction requirements shape what is possible.

The existing condominium stock is a market of its own. Buildings put up decades ago need work on roofs, envelopes, balconies and structural elements, and that work sits at the intersection of an association, its unit owners and a contractor, with genuine disagreement about who is responsible for what.

How Ready Legal helps

Ready Legal works with owners, associations, contractors and subcontractors on construction matters. That includes drafting and negotiating construction contracts before work begins, and handling payment disputes, lien claims, defect claims and construction litigation when a project goes wrong. That includes disputes involving multi-unit and association-governed buildings.

Local businesses

The town's economy runs on tourism, surf and beach retail, restaurants and the trades that support seasonal property. Ready Legal also represents businesses on formation, contracts, commercial disputes and collections.

Courts serving New Smyrna Beach

New Smyrna Beach sits in Volusia County, which is served by Florida's Seventh Judicial Circuit. Litigation arising out of New Smyrna Beach disputes is generally handled in Volusia County. Ready Legal's office is in downtown Orlando and the firm is available to represent clients in matters in the Seventh Judicial Circuit.
