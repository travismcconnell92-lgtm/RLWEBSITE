Construction & Business Attorney Serving Melbourne

Melbourne's construction market is driven by industry rather than tourism, which makes it unusual on this coast.

Ready Legal's office is in downtown Orlando, Florida. We do not maintain an office in Melbourne. Ready Legal represents clients throughout Central Florida, including Melbourne and the rest of Brevard County.

Building in Melbourne

Aerospace, defense and engineering employers have built and expanded manufacturing, laboratory and office facilities in the area for decades, and that work continues. Industrial and technical buildings carry requirements that ordinary commercial construction does not: power, environmental controls, clean space, security, and long-lead equipment that has to be coordinated with the building around it.

Alongside that, sustained employment growth has produced substantial residential and commercial construction across the county. The result is a market where a subcontractor may be working on a production facility one month and a subdivision the next.

How Ready Legal helps

Ready Legal works with owners, contractors and subcontractors on commercial and industrial construction matters. That includes drafting and negotiating construction contracts before work begins, and handling payment disputes, lien claims, defect claims and construction litigation when a project goes wrong.

Melbourne businesses

The area supports a deep base of engineering, manufacturing, technical and trade companies, many of them working as suppliers or subcontractors to larger firms. Ready Legal also represents businesses on formation, contracts, commercial disputes and collections.

Courts serving Melbourne

Melbourne sits in Brevard County, which is served by Florida's Eighteenth Judicial Circuit. Litigation arising out of Melbourne disputes is generally handled in Brevard County. Ready Legal's office is in downtown Orlando and the firm is available to represent clients in matters in the Eighteenth Judicial Circuit.
