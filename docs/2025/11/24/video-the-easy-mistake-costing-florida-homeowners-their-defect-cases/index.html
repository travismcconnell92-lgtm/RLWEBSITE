Video: The Easy Mistake Costing Florida Homeowners Their Defect Cases

November 24, 2025 · 1 min read · Travis S. McConnell

Most Florida homeowners assume that proving a construction defect is enough to win, and it is not. Florida courts require solid, legally compliant damages evidence, and without it even a strong defect claim can collapse on appeal. This video explains why expert testimony is critical, what the expert must actually prove, and how the Bandklayder case shows the consequences of getting it wrong.
