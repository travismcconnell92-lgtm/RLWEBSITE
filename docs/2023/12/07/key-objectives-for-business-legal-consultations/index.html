Key Objectives for Business Legal Consultations

December 7, 2023 · 2 min read · Travis S. McConnell

Meeting with your business's attorney is an important, and often expensive necessity in operating your business. With that in mind, it is helpful to approach these meetings with a strategy to obtain legal advice in the most effective and efficient manner possible. Whether you find yourself entangled in a contract dispute or contemplating a significant business decision, this article outlines three key goals for meeting with your attorney.

Goal 1: Determine Your Business's Rights and Obligations

The first objective in meeting with your business attorney is to ascertain your rights concerning the subject matter at hand. For instance, if you are considering terminating a contract, understanding your rights to do so is important. Your attorney can analyze the contract, providing insights into the termination provisions and any conditions that might trigger your right to exit the agreement.

By delving into the nuances of the contract, your attorney helps you decipher and identify any grounds for exiting it. This allows you to make informed decisions aligned with your business's rights, minimizing the risk of unintended consequences.

Goal 2: Determine the Consequences of Your Legal Course of Action

Knowing your rights is essential, but equally critical is understanding the potential repercussions of exercising those rights. In our example of contract termination, your attorney will outline the consequences your business might face if you terminate the contract without the appropriate grounds. This involves assessing any financial liabilities, penalties, or legal actions the other party could pursue.

This knowledge allows you to weigh the benefits against the risks, preventing impulsive decisions that could have severe financial or legal consequences. Armed with a understanding of potential pitfalls, you can make strategic choices that align with your business's long-term interests.

Goal 3: Determine the Recommended Course of Action

With a clear understanding of your rights and the consequences tied to them, the next crucial objective is to determine the recommended course of action. Your attorney, drawing on their legal expertise and experience, can provide valuable insights into the optimal strategy based on the unique circumstances of your case.

For instance, even if the contract is enforceable and carries strict penalties, your attorney might advise you that the risk of the other party successfully enforcing those penalties is minimal. This risk-benefit analysis helps tailor the legal advice to your specific situation, ensuring that the chosen course of action is optimized for the best outcome.

Closing

In essence, a consultation with your business attorney revolves around three key objectives: understanding your rights, grasping the consequences of exercising those rights, and determining the recommended course of action. By pursuing these objectives, you empower your business to make informed, strategic decisions in the face of legal challenges.
